ABOUT

We build trust between people.

PROFILE

細田 卓次郎Takujiro Hosoda

株式会社 築大 代表取締役CEO, Tsukidai Co., Ltd.

1983年東京都生まれ。筑波大学体育専門学群卒業。在学中は水泳部競泳に所属し、競技活動を通じてスポーツが持つ人材育成と、人と人を結ぶ力を学ぶ。

Born in Tokyo in 1983. Graduated from the School of Health and Physical Education, University of Tsukuba, where he swam competitively for the university team — and learned first-hand how sport develops people and binds them to one another.

大学卒業後、株式会社三井住友銀行に入行。金融の現場でビジネスの基礎を培うとともに、社会人となってからも競泳を続け、日本実業団水泳競技大会に三井住友銀行所属選手として出場。

After graduating he joined Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, building his foundations in business on the front line of finance while continuing to compete, representing the bank at the Japan Corporate Swimming Championships.

その後、株式会社 築大の経営に参画し、現在は代表取締役として、宿泊事業を基盤にスポーツ・教育・国際交流・産学連携を横断した事業に取り組む。

He later joined the management of Tsukidai, and as CEO now leads work spanning sport, education, international exchange and industry–academia collaboration, built on the company's hospitality business.

母校である筑波大学では、体育スポーツ局 プラチナアソシエイトとして活動。また、筑波大学附属駒場中学校・高等学校 水泳部監督、グローバル部／ビジネス部 社会連携ディレクターとして、スポーツ・教育を軸に、大学・企業・大使館・海外教育機関等との社会連携・国際交流活動に携わる。

At his alma mater he serves as a Platinum Associate of the Bureau of Physical Education and Sports, University of Tsukuba. He is also head coach of the swimming team at the Junior and Senior High School at Komaba, University of Tsukuba, and Director of Social Collaboration for its Global and Business departments — working with universities, companies, embassies and overseas educational institutions through sport and education.

筑波大学で培った「自他共栄」の精神、金融機関で培ったビジネスの視点、そして経営の経験を掛け合わせ、「人と人、教育と社会、日本と世界をつなぐ」ことで、新たな価値を社会に「築く」ことを目指している。

Combining the principle of jita-kyoei — mutual prosperity for self and others — learned at Tsukuba, the commercial perspective gained in banking, and his experience in management, he aims to build new value for society by connecting people to people, education to society, and Japan to the world.
